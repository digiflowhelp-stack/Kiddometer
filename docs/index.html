How it all combines

The video is split into 60-second segments. Each segment gets the age its strictest metric demands. The overall rating is the 90th percentile across segments: sustained intensity decides and a single isolated peak does not dominate — those peaks are listed separately as "most intense moments". A flash-limit violation always raises a warning.
